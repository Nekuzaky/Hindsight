# Code of Conduct

Hindsight is a small, friendly project. Everyone taking part, in issues, pull requests or discussions, agrees to:

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's experience, background or identity.
- Give and take feedback about code, not about people.
- Stay on topic and avoid spam or self-promotion.

Harassment, insults and discriminatory language are not tolerated. Maintainers may edit or remove content and
block participants who break these rules.

To report a problem privately, contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile: https://github.com/Nekuzaky
